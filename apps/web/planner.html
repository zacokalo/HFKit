What the numbers are
Each cell is an SNR margin in dB: the signal-to-noise ratio the engine predicts for that band at that hour, minus the 24 dB in 3 kHz a voice contact needs. Above zero is workable; above ten is comfortable.
It is not a probability. The engine's reliability column is a known defect and is not shown. The calibrated score the design docs describe does not exist yet.
Isotropic antennas at both ends, so a real station with a decent antenna does better than this table. The comms planner adds your antenna's pattern on top.
Quiet geomagnetic conditions are assumed. When Kp is high, the higher bands and polar paths will read optimistic.
